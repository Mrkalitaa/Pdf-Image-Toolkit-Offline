[FIELD: Description]
Pdf Image Toolkit: offline is the fast, modern, and completely private Windows utility for everyday document and photo tasks. Designed specifically for students, educators, office staff, accountants, and small business owners who need dependable tools without recurring subscriptions, cloud uploads, or account logins.

100% OFFLINE & PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Unlike cloud-based web tools where your confidential bank statements, contracts, tax documents, and personal photos are uploaded to third-party servers, Pdf Image Toolkit: offline runs entirely on your local PC. Files never leave your computer, no network calls are ever made by our processing engine, and there are zero analytics, zero telemetry, and zero ads.

COMPREHENSIVE PDF TOOLS
1. Merge PDFs: Combine multiple PDF documents into a single organized file with intuitive drag-and-drop page reordering.
2. Split PDFs: Extract custom page ranges (e.g., "1-3, 5, 8-10"), split every individual page, or split every N pages into equal chunks.
3. Compress PDFs (Dual-Mode):
   - Light Mode: Stream re-encoding and metadata stripping; preserves 100% searchable text.
   - Strong Mode: High-performance rasterization with DPI selection (96, 150, 200 DPI) and JPEG quality control.
   - Guaranteed non-expansion: If compression produces a larger file, your original file is automatically retained.
4. Rotate Pages: Orient scanned pages by 90°, 180°, or 270° across all or selected pages.
5. Images to PDF: Convert JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and Apple HEIC photos into multi-page PDF documents with A4, Letter, or Fit-to-Image sizing and custom margins.
6. PDF to Images: Rasterize entire PDFs or specific page ranges into high-resolution PNG or JPG images at up to 300 DPI.

HIGH-PERFORMANCE IMAGE TOOLS
7. Compress Images: Batch compress photos with live file size savings tracking and quality slider. Optional toggle to strip camera EXIF metadata (GPS location, camera serials, timestamps).
8. Resize Images: Batch scale images by percentage (10% to 200%) or exact pixel dimensions with aspect ratio lock to prevent distortion. Includes one-click standard presets: 1080x1080 (Square/Social), 1280x720 (HD 720p), and 1920x1080 (Full HD 1080p).
9. Convert Formats: Fast batch conversion across JPG, PNG, WebP, and Apple HEIC/HEIF with built-in Windows Imaging Component fallback.

BUILT FOR MODERN WINDOWS
- Native Windows 11 Fluent Design: Features Mica material backdrop and seamless Light/Dark mode synchronization.
- Non-Blocking Performance: Built with C# and .NET 8 on Windows App SDK with background asynchronous processing.
- Non-Destructive Auto-Naming: Never overwrites original files (automatically adds smart suffixes like doc_compressed.pdf).
- Accessible: Full keyboard navigation and accessible screen reader labeling throughout.
