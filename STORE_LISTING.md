## 3. Full Description

**Pdf Image Toolkit: offline** is the fast, modern, and completely private Windows utility for everyday document and photo tasks. Designed specifically for students, educators, office staff, accountants, and small business owners who need dependable tools without recurring subscriptions, cloud uploads, or account logins.

### 🛡️ 100% Offline & Private by Design
Unlike cloud-based web tools where your confidential bank statements, contracts, tax documents, and personal photos are uploaded to third-party servers, **Pdf Image Toolkit: offline runs entirely on your local PC**. Files never leave your computer, no network calls are ever made by our processing engine, and there are zero analytics, zero telemetry, and zero ads.

---

### 📄 Comprehensive PDF Tools

1. **Merge PDFs**:
   - Combine multiple PDF documents into a single organized file.
   - Drag and drop files to reorder pages and view total page counts and file sizes instantly.
2. **Split PDFs**:
   - Extract custom page ranges using intuitive syntax (e.g., `1-3, 5, 8-10`).
   - Split every individual page into its own document.
   - Split every N pages into equal document chunks.
3. **Compress PDFs (Dual-Mode)**:
   - **Light Compression**: Re-encodes internal PDF data streams and strips unneeded metadata while preserving 100% selectable, searchable text.
   - **Strong Compression**: Rasterizes documents at chosen DPI (96, 150, or 200 DPI) with adjustable JPEG quality for maximum file size reduction.
   - Real-time before-and-after size reporting with guaranteed non-expansion (if compression produces a larger file, your original file size is retained).
4. **Rotate Pages**:
   - Orient scanned documents and upside-down pages by 90°, 180°, or 270°.
   - Rotate all pages at once or target specific page numbers.
5. **Images to PDF**:
   - Convert JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and HEIC photos into clean, multi-page PDF documents.
   - Choose standard page sizes (A4, US Letter, or Fit to Image) with customizable margins.
6. **PDF to Images**:
   - Rasterize entire PDF documents or specific page ranges into high-resolution PNG or JPG image files.
   - Select output resolution from 96 DPI (web), 150 DPI (standard), 200 DPI (high readability), to 300 DPI (print quality).

---

### 🖼️ High-Performance Image Tools

7. **Compress Images**:
   - Batch-compress photos with an intuitive quality slider (1 to 100).
   - View exact bytes saved and percentage reduction per file.
   - Toggle to strip EXIF and camera metadata (GPS locations, camera serials, timestamps).
8. **Resize Images**:
   - Scale batch images by percentage (10% to 200%).
   - Resize to exact pixel dimensions with aspect ratio lock to prevent distortion.
   - One-click presets: `1080 × 1080` (Square / Social), `1280 × 720` (HD 720p), `1920 × 1080` (Full HD 1080p).
9. **Convert Formats**:
   - Convert between popular web and graphic formats: JPG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC.
   - Built-in Windows Imaging Component (WIC) fallback for iPhone / iPad HEIC images.
   - Option to clean sensitive metadata upon conversion.
